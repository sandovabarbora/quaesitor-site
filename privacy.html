Privacy, and what this practice is not.

Two different things live on this page. What happens to your data — on the site and during an engagement — and what our verdict does and does not stand for. Both are short, because there is not much to declare.

Enquiries, the thirty minute call, and any request about your own data all reach barbora@quaesitor.eu, and it is answered by the person who signs the work. We are the controller for anything you send there.

What the website collects

Nothing. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no embedded third parties. Every font, image and script is inside the page file, so opening it makes no request to anyone but the server that served it. There is no form to submit, so there is nothing here that could collect anything by accident either.

Our host records the requests it serves, as any web server does. We do not read those logs to build a picture of who visited, and nothing on the page adds to them.

If you write to us

What you send by email is processed to answer you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to an enquiry (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). It arrives in a mailbox on this domain with no scheduling service in between, so who asks for a call is not visible to a third party. Kept while the conversation is live and up to two years after, then deleted.

  • Access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability
  • Complaint to the Czech Data Protection Authority (ÚOOÚ)
  • No profiling, no automated decisions, no sale or transfer

During a review

The suite runs against your warehouse, in your infrastructure, under your credentials, and no data of yours reaches a model account of ours. Where an engagement puts us in contact with personal data it runs under a processing agreement signed before any access is granted, and every sub-processor is named in it.

What we keep afterwards is the questions, the queries and the results — the material the report is built from. If any of it carries personal data, the processing agreement says how long it is held and when it is destroyed, and that agreement takes precedence over this page.

What this is not

This is a technical review of what an AI system answers over your data. It is not a statutory audit under Act 93/2009 Coll. — we are not registered auditors, and nothing we issue stands in for an auditor's report or an opinion on your financial statements. It is not an accredited certification: no certificate is issued and no accreditation is claimed. Where the AI Act requires a conformity assessment for a high-risk system, that is reserved for a notified body and is not what this is.

What we issue is a verification statement: our own finding, signed and dated, naming what was measured, what the result was, and what it does not cover. Its value is that the measurement behind it can be repeated by anyone holding the suite — not that a regulator stands behind it.
