Findings of the review of the warehouse assistant

anonymised composite · 26 questions · 3 repeats · queries handed over

1. Summary

The assistant's correct answers are not the story; both models tested return the same number of them. They differ in what they do when they cannot answer: one declines, the other returns a well-formatted wrong number. The findings below are the second kind.

2. Representative finding: F-07

silent failure cohort definition
QUESTION
"How many customers made a second purchase within 30 days of their first?"
ASSISTANT
684 customers. The cohort was anchored on the registration date, not the first purchase. The SQL is syntactically correct and ran without error.
VERIFIED
621 customers. Definition owned by Head of BI, stated in writing; query read against it clause by clause; anchored to the closed-month retention report.
DIVERGENCE
10.1%, reproducible across repeats. Nothing downstream carried a signal that the number should not be trusted.

3. Fix and retest

  1. Anchor cohort questions in the semantic layer to the first-purchase date; the written definition is included as Appendix A.
  2. Add the naive/verified query pair to your regression suite; both are handed over with this report.
  3. The retest of remediated findings is included in the engagement.
